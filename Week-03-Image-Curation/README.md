## Identity Kit

**Fonts**

- Heading: Lora
- Body: Inter

**Palette**

- Main: #2D6A4F (deep teal-green)
- Text: #1B1B1B (near-black)
- Background: #FAFAF8 (near-white)
- Accent: #E9C46A (warm gold) — buttons/highlights only, not body text (low contrast on near-white background).

**Logo / Favicon**

A simple monogram: initials in Lora, deep teal-green on near-white, in a circular or square badge.

**Style Note**

Warm, reassuring, and calm — Lora headings give a human, editorial feel while Inter keeps body text clean and easy to read; the teal-green and warm gold combination feels trustworthy without being corporate, so the work itself stays the loudest thing on the page.

## Portfolio Demonstration — Fictional Customer-Support Inbox

Because my supervisor did not grant permission to use screenshots of the actual workplace customer-service system, I chose not to use company screenshots or customer information.

Instead, I created a fictional customer-support inbox illustration for portfolio demonstration purposes. It uses invented customer details and a fictional conversation to show how an AI-assisted reply can be reviewed and improved.

The illustration demonstrates:

- A fictional customer-support inbox and customer message.
- A generic initial response compared with a warmer, more helpful reply.
- Human review of tone, accuracy and personalisation before a reply is sent.

**Important distinction:** The inbox, customer names and conversation are fictional. They are not screenshots or records from HealthGarde, and the illustration does not independently prove workplace performance.

### Actual Work Experience

In my customer-support work, my typical response time improved from approximately **20–30 minutes to 10–12 minutes per customer email**.

I reviewed and edited AI-assisted replies before sending them. I checked the tone and accuracy and made changes where needed so the responses remained appropriate and human.

These response-time figures reflect my actual work experience. The fictional inbox illustration is a separate visual demonstration.
